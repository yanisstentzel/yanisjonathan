The project

Why we run

Millions of children around the world grow up without a safe home. Run 4 Smiles brings four countries together, France, Latvia, Ukraine and South Korea, for a single day of sport, music and solidarity.

All the money raised from tickets, merchandise and online donations goes to programmes that support homeless children, in partnership with UNICEF.

  1. 01

    Online first

    The campaign starts online: registrations, donations and a virtual run anyone can join from anywhere.

  2. 02

    Race by day

    On race day, thousands of runners take to the streets of each capital.

  3. 03

    Concert by night

    Each city ends the day with a concert by major artists from that country.

FAQ

Questions

Who can take part in the race?

Everyone aged 16 and over can run the 10 km. A kids race is organised for younger participants.

I don't live in one of the four cities. Can I join?

Yes! Sign up for the virtual run and complete your distance wherever you are.

Where does the money go?

All profits from tickets, merchandise and donations go to programmes supporting homeless children, in partnership with UNICEF.

Who is playing at the concerts?

The line-up for each city will be announced soon.
